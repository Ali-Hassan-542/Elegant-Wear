Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about our products and services.

What is your return policy?

We offer a 7-day return policy for unused items with original tags attached. Returns are accepted for store credit or exchange. Sale items are final sale and cannot be returned.

How long does shipping take?

Standard shipping within Pakistan takes 3-5 business days. Express shipping is available for delivery within 1-2 business days. International shipping times vary by destination.

Do you offer custom sizing?

Yes, we offer custom sizing for most of our unstitched collections. Please contact our customer service team with your measurements at least 7 days before you need the outfit.

Can I visit your physical store?

Absolutely! Our flagship store is located at 123 Fashion Street, Clifton, Karachi. We'd love to help you in person with styling advice and fabric selection.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes, we ship to over 50 countries worldwide. International shipping costs and delivery times vary by location. You can calculate shipping costs at checkout.

How do I care for my Elegant Wear clothing?

Each product comes with specific care instructions. Generally, we recommend dry cleaning for embroidered pieces and hand washing for delicate fabrics. Avoid direct sunlight when drying.
