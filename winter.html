Winter Wear Care Tips

Proper Storage

Store winter clothes in breathable garment bags to prevent moisture buildup and maintain fabric quality.

Cleaning

Dry clean velvet and wool garments to preserve their texture and color. Spot clean when possible.

Airing

Air out your winter clothes after wearing to remove moisture and keep them fresh between wears.
